# Everglades milestone artwork

`everglades-reveal.jpg` is the artwork for the second milestone, reached at €800 in total donations. It depicts a crocodile and an iconic Everglades airboat with a large rear propeller cage, using the site's navy, blue and violet twilight palette. It is generated campaign artwork, not footage from the trip.

Title: **Everglades**

Description: **Dodamies meklēt krokodilus — cerams, izdosies tos arī nofilmēt!**

The title, description, image and alt text are configured in `donations/campaign-goals.js`. The existing shared renderer displays the locked placeholder below €800 and reveals this content when the donation total reaches €800. Live donation updates trigger the same renderer, so no further content edit is needed at the threshold. This is a visual reveal on a static site; its source files are public.

Created with the built-in imagegen tool in two passes. The final PNG is exported to JPEG for the site.

## Initial generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: final portrait 3:4 artwork for the second unlocked milestone on a Florida travel fundraising website.
Primary request: an atmospheric Everglades wildlife adventure reveal, focused on spotting and filming a wild crocodile.
Create a cinematic, realistic wildlife travel poster: a single American crocodile with a naturally narrow tapered snout, eyes and textured scaly head clearly visible above the still water, upper body partly visible, swimming quietly in an Everglades mangrove channel at blue hour. Low water-level three-quarter perspective. The crocodile is the clear focal point, head and eye in the upper-middle half, large enough to recognize in a tiny portrait card. Reeds and mangrove roots frame the scene, distant low wetland vegetation, a little mist over the channel, restrained pink-violet twilight at the horizon. Natural wildlife observation mood, suspenseful and adventurous, calm animal rather than an attack. No people needed.
Lighting/style: match the existing website artwork: deep midnight navy shadows, cool electric blue and turquoise reflections in the water, restrained violet/pink rim highlights, soft cinematic dusk light on the animal with legible natural texture. Premium realistic photographic/3D cinematic finish. Cohesive navy, indigo, teal and violet palette, not bright daytime greens or orange sunset. Soft dark framing at the edges.
Composition: portrait 3:4, ideally 1086 x 1448. Keep the animal's entire head and its eye within the central 75% width and upper 55% height. Its snout must not be cropped off. Receding tail/water can occupy lower areas, bottom quarter fades smoothly into deep navy #06111a for the website's title and caption overlay. No important subject in bottom quarter.
Constraints: one anatomically credible crocodile, no giant monster, no bared-teeth attack pose, no extra limbs, no city skyline, no text, no lettering, no watermark, no badge, no lock or question mark. This is illustrative campaign artwork, not footage of an already completed trip.
